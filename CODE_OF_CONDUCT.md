# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of Bunny.ai pledge to make participation in our project and our community a respectful, welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behaviour

- Demonstrate empathy, kindness, and respect toward others.
- Be constructive when offering or receiving technical critique and feedback.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and personal context.
- Prioritize safe, honest defaults and open-source collaboration.
- Focus on what is best for the overall project and user community.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or exclusionary comments.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, insults, or derogatory remarks.
- Publishing private personal data, credentials, or private communication without explicit consent.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, advances, or inappropriate conduct.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative professional setting.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or offensive.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the maintainers at `conduct@bunny.ai` (or via private maintainer contact listed on the repository profile). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by project leadership.
